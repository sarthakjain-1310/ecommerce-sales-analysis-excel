# E-commerce Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce sales dataset using **Microsoft Excel** to identify sales, profit, customer, product, category, and discount-related trends.

The project includes data cleaning, analysis, KPI creation, PivotTables, slicers, charts, and an interactive dashboard.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

* **Total Orders:** 12,035
* **Total Sales:** ₹272,534,006
* **Total Profit:** ₹45,003,295
* **Tool Used:** Microsoft Excel

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The dashboard analyzes:

* Monthly sales and profit trends
* Category-wise sales and profit margin
* Customer-wise sales and profit
* Product performance
* Discount impact on sales and profit
* Key performance indicators (KPIs)
* Top-performing and low-performing products/customers

## 💡 Key Insights

* August recorded the highest sales, while July recorded the lowest.
* Electronics generated the highest sales, with a profit margin of approximately 15%.
* Fashion had the lowest sales among the major categories but maintained a higher profit margin of approximately 25%.
* Beauty recorded approximately 29% profit margin.
* Home & Kitchen recorded approximately 22% profit margin.
* Overall sales increased by approximately 1% across the two-year period, while profit margin remained relatively stable.
* Some customers generated high sales but were not among the highest-profit customers, highlighting the importance of analyzing both revenue and profitability.
* High-selling products did not always generate the highest profit because of differences in profit margins.
* Higher discounts can significantly affect profitability and should be evaluated alongside sales growth.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to demonstrate practical **Excel data analysis and dashboard-building skills** by converting raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.
